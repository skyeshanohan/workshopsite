HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTO
=====================

1. Save a square headshot as photo.jpg (recommended size: 800×800 or larger).
2. Put that file in this public/ folder (next to this text file).
3. Open src/data/profile.js and set:

     photo: '/photo.jpg',

4. Save the file, then refresh the local site (npm run dev).

To hide the photo and show initials instead, set:

     photo: '',

Do not publish photos of other people without permission.
